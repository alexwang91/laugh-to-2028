# BRRK-0007 Factor Ensemble + CYCLE-0002 — 2026-08-04

## BRRK-MVP-0007-FACTOR-ENSEMBLE

Preregistered before first run. Frozen V1 alpha, four semantic states, BRRK-0006 authority structure, 30-day refit, 20-day/5000-scenario meta model, 20% tail-risk budget, 5 bps cost. The only structural change was equal-weight averaging of three independently fitted state models using PCA factor counts 3, 4, and 5. No member received a PnL-derived weight.

Decision: the equal ensemble does not replace frozen BRRK-0006 on headline performance. However, the experiment is retained as strong evidence of material model-specification uncertainty. Selecting PCA4 solely because it has the best realized PnL would be post-selection bias and is prohibited.

### Model-specification diagnostics

- mean ensemble scale: 0.8062
- mean scale range across the 3/4/5 models: 0.1466
- mean posterior entropy of the averaged semantic posterior: 0.3682
- individual mean posterior entropy:
  - PCA3: 0.0186
  - PCA4: 0.00775
  - PCA5: 0.00561
- all three model families converged at every decision.

The individual HMMs are therefore nearly hard classifiers, but the specification ensemble reveals much larger uncertainty.

At the final registered monthly decision, 2026-07-21:
- PCA3: P(RISK_OFF)=0.038%, P(BTC_LEAD)=99.961%, final scale=1.0
- PCA4: P(RISK_OFF)=99.040%, final scale=0.00960
- PCA5: P(RISK_OFF)=99.990%, final scale=0.000103
- equal ensemble P(RISK_OFF)=66.356%, final scale=0.33657
- member final-scale range=0.99990

This is a first-order implementation risk: the same data lead to opposite trade states under small, preregistered changes in the representation dimension. A single-model posterior near 0% or 100% must not be interpreted as equivalent to true model certainty.

## CYCLE-0002-SYMMETRIC-CUSUM

Preregistered before first run. Same standardized CUSUM as CYCLE-0001: k=0.5, h=5, 60-day volatility normalization. The only change was removal of the EMA20 confirmation; a positive CUSUM alarm alone released suppression back to frozen V1.

Result: identical to CYCLE-0001.

| V1 | 61.25% | -37.63% | 1.295 | 1.628 | 36.55% |

Historical timing is also unchanged:
- 2018: arm 2018-11-16 / 2018-11-20; release 2019-05-11.
- 2022: arm 2022-06-13 / 2022-11-09; release 2023-01-13.
- 2026-08-02: CAPITULATION_ARMED, not released.

Conclusion: EMA confirmation was not causing the slow recovery. The positive CUSUM itself requires too much accumulated positive drift. Do not tune k/h on the same window. Further cycle-reentry research must use a structurally different sequential model.

## Research decision

1. Frozen BRRK-0006 remains the headline candidate but its model-specification risk is now explicitly recognized.
2. BRRK-0007 is not promoted as a replacement, but its uncertainty diagnostics should be added to automated shadow reporting.
3. The next state-model experiment should target persistent semantic identity across rolling refits rather than select a factor count by PnL.
4. CUSUM reentry is stopped after CYCLE-0002; no threshold optimization is permitted on this window.
5. Future downside-risk work should target asymmetric downside moments / calibration of risk forecasts, not another symmetric volatility gate.
