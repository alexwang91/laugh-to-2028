## Interpretation

Persistent latent identity is not automatically equivalent to persistent economic meaning. In this crypto feature system, component geometry and market structure drift enough that forcing historical identity continuity can preserve the wrong economic label. The latest Wasserstein-HMM literature motivates identity stability, but this low-degree-of-freedom economic-template implementation does not validate that approach for the present strategy.

The deeper lesson is that the trading gate should not depend on the arbitrary semantic name of one HMM component if an invariant alternative is available. The next experiment therefore removes semantic labels from the trading decision: raw states remain raw states, and the gate is based on the posterior probability that the current raw state belongs to a state whose V1 conditional expected return is negative, with estimation uncertainty explicitly incorporated.

No 0008 distance weighting or template parameter will be tuned on this window.
