## Exact structural rule

Frozen V1 alpha remains unchanged.

BRRK-0005 produces a one-dimensional V1 meta exposure `meta_scale` from the state-conditioned historical return distribution of V1 itself.

BRRK-0006 changes only the authority of that meta allocator:

`final_scale = 1 - P(RISK_OFF) * (1 - meta_scale)`

- non-RISK_OFF posterior -> default to V1 full scale;
- RISK_OFF posterior -> meta allocator may de-risk;
- intermediate posterior -> continuous blend;
- no arbitrary probability threshold.

All other state-model, shrinkage, scenario, risk-budget, rebalance and cost assumptions remain those preregistered for 0005.

## No-lookahead audit note

The price-only HMM semantic mapping labels raw latent states using contemporaneous training features only: BTC trend, major/alt breadth, relative strength and drawdown. It does not use future return labels. Decision probabilities are generated with a forward filter `P(S_t | X_1:t)`. This removes the specific future-label look-ahead concern from the state decision path.

## Why this likely worked

0004/0005 showed that high-volatility bull states have high V1 alpha and should not automatically be de-risked. 0005 also found a materially negative historical V1 daily mean in the semantic RISK_OFF state while BTC_LEAD, MAJOR_ROTATION and ALT_EXPANSION had positive V1 means.

0006 therefore gives the risk model a narrow job: suppress V1 only when the state model believes the market is in RISK_OFF. This preserved almost all V1 upside capture while reducing downside participation.

## Required validation before promotion

Do not tune 0006 further on the common window. Next work should be validation only:

1. subperiod and start-date stability;
2. transaction-cost stress;
3. risk-overlay execution-delay stress;
4. sensitivity to small preregistered modelling perturbations (state count / PCA count / refit interval), reported as a full family rather than selecting the winner;
5. block-bootstrap / probabilistic comparison of 0006 versus V1;
6. DSR and PBO/CSCV across the complete experiment family;
7. if possible, a genuinely untouched temporal or cross-universe holdout.

Promotion to a live candidate requires robustness across these tests. The 2022-2026 headline result alone is insufficient because model architecture selection used information learned from earlier experiments on the same window.
