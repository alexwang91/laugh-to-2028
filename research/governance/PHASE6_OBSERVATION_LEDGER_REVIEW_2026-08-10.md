## Competing hypotheses

### H1 — Green workflow alone is enough for credit

Rejected. Frozen evidence rules require durable evidence plus a separate receipt; the ledger validator therefore requires both identities and their binding.

### H2 — A repository ledger can itself create Phase-6 credit

Rejected and structurally forbidden. The ledger declares `recording_creates_credit=false`, is explicitly `ACCOUNTING_INDEX_NON_EVIDENCE`, and the validator fails if those invariants change.

### H3 — Recording an already-persisted event later is historical backfill

Rejected only under the narrow facts above. No observation, raw response, decision timestamp, evidence object or receipt is generated by the ledger. The ledger may index an already-existing durable event. A missed timestamp without prior durable evidence cannot be created this way.

### H4 — Manual dispatch or rerun may be substituted for a missing schedule

Rejected. Scheduled-credit entries must have `event_name=schedule`; decision timestamps and run/attempt identities must be unique. Manual emergency drills remain a different acceptance dimension.

### H5 — Empty alerts alone establish all closeout requirements

Rejected. Empty alerts plus exact target-reference parity support the per-observation zero-error accounting only. They do not establish the 14-day elapsed threshold, 10 scheduled decisions or required emergency drill.

### H6 — This accounting change can unlock Phase 7 or production

Rejected. The validator fails if production, signing or order-submission authority becomes true. Phase-6 live acceptance remains `MEASUREMENT_INCONCLUSIVE_TIME_DEPENDENT`.

## Remaining uncertainty

The repository validator cannot independently query GitHub Actions during offline CI to prove that an external artifact still exists. This is intentional: letting the static ledger self-prove its external evidence would be circular. Human/reviewer evidence verification remains required when adding each entry; the Actions artifacts and receipt are the evidence authority.

Artifact expiry before acceptance review remains governed by the frozen evidence contract. If required evidence expires before acceptance review, the associated decision ceases to be creditable even if an old ledger row remains. Therefore closeout must re-check artifact availability rather than trust row count alone.

## Result

The first observation may be indexed as scheduled decision #1, subject to PR CI and review, without changing its underlying evidence or creating retroactive observation credit.

Phase-6 live acceptance: **NOT PASSED / MEASUREMENT_INCONCLUSIVE_TIME_DEPENDENT**.
